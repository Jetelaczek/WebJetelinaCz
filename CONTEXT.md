# jetelina.cz

Tomáš Jetelina's personal website: an online CV, in English and Czech, that presents his professional background to people considering working with him.

## Language

### People

**Visitor**:
Someone reading the site, typically an international recruiter or hiring manager. Their intended next steps are to send a Contact message first and open the CV second.
_Avoid_: User, customer, client

### Content

**Headline**:
Tomáš's one professional title, "Identity & Cloud Automation Engineer", used identically on the page, in the browser tab and in both CVs.
_Avoid_: Tagline, job title (when it means something else in different places)

**Career data**:
The single, authoritative record of Tomáš's roles, employers, projects and skills. The page and both CVs are derived from it, and LinkedIn is maintained separately by hand.
_Avoid_: Profile, resume data

**Project**:
A piece of work Tomáš builds or maintains, described in plain language without naming the underlying repository. Shown as a Project card under "Projects I've Been Working On", with the Active Directory experience always the last card.
_Avoid_: Repo, repository, current project

**Employer**:
A company Tomáš has worked for, shown as a card under "Where I've Worked" with what the company does, his role(s) and the dates.
_Avoid_: Company I've been working on, client

**CV**:
The downloadable PDF version of the Career data, one per language, opened in a new tab.
_Avoid_: Resume, résumé, životopis (in English text)

**Contact message**:
A message a Visitor sends through the site's form, delivered to Tomáš's inbox.
_Avoid_: Enquiry, lead
